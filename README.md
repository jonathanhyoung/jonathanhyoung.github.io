# jonathanhyoung.github.io

For now, a showcase of some useful hacks:

<https://jonathanhyoung.github.io/eggshaker.html> Turn your phone into an egg shaker.

<https://jonathanhyoung.github.io/sandblock.html> Turn your phone into a sand block.

<https://jonathanhyoung.github.io/transcriber/> Turn your phone into a closed-caption device.

<https://jonathanhyoung.github.io/random-bartletts.html> Get a random quotation from Barlett's Familiar Quotations every time you reload.
